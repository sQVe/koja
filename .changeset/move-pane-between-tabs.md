Add `koja pane move <id>` with one of `--tab <id>`, `--new-tab`, or `--new-workspace`. The pane
keeps its process and its id, and takes the focus at its destination. `--new-workspace` roots the
workspace at the pane's current folder. In Koja mode, `!` moves the focused pane to a new tab in its
workspace.
