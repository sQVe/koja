Show a role badge's full label while the pane header has room. When space runs out, the folder and
branch give up their space first, then a long label such as `integration-test-runner` cuts at the
end with an ellipsis and shows in full on hover. Built-in role names stay whole while the header has
room; when the controls need the space, the badge cuts below the built-in width so they stay in the
pane, and the full name stays in the tooltip. The pane name keeps its space. A narrow pane header
switches to the kit's compact pane actions button. That button stays visible and its menu lists the
pane's task, branch, and folder above the four actions.
