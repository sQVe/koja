Round every corner Koja draws with one 6 px radius: pane and side panel frames, tabs, inset row
pills, buttons, icon toggles, and the mode pill. Badges keep 4 px, and a card's inner corner follows
its frame. The kit's pane header, which the mock scenes draw, no longer paints a focus fill: in
insert mode the pane border and the brighter title mark focus, and normal mode keeps its tint.
