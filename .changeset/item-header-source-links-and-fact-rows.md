Add the content pane's item header, source links, and fact rows to the kit. The header draws the
item's identifier as a link naming its source, its title, a state chip, and one age, and a loading
item draws only the identifier and its source. Facts draw as rows with one label column, or as pairs
that wrap under the title. The kit also gains an open pull request icon. The kit gallery's Content
section shows each part, with a long ticket title and its wrapped facts.
