The `catppuccin-latte-muted` theme's text and status marks now meet WCAG 2.2 AA contrast. The "Needs
you" badge label draws in the dark foreground color. The diff hunk header, the diff line numbers,
and the disabled action button label use the darker muted color. A hovered solid or danger button
draws a ring instead of fading its fill and label.
