Lead each tab and pane header with the mark that says why it needs you: the question bubble for an
ask, the check for an unseen finished turn, and the spinner while an agent works. A tab or header
with nothing to say keeps its program icon. The mark takes the program icon's place, so neither
surface draws a second mark at its end.

The kit also draws a red triangle alert for a crash. It shows once a crash fact reaches the kit.
