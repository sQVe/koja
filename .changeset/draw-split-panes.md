Draw every pane of the focused tab in the terminal client at its layout rectangle. When a tab has
two or more panes, each pane gets its own thin frame with one blank column between side-by-side
frames, and the focused pane's frame is drawn in an accent color. A tab with one pane has no frame
and fills the whole area. Resizing the terminal redraws each pane at its new size.
