Add the content pane's sections, status rows, and part notes to the kit. A section draws a
collapsible header with its name and a dim count, and every part starts at the label's edge. A
status row draws a mark in a fixed slot, the name, a detail line under it at the same indent, and a
state word; a row of a part that failed to refresh keeps its mark still. A part note names the part,
its age, and Retry. The kit gallery's Content section shows a pull request's checks, threads, and
slices, live and stale.
