Add the content pane's sections, status rows, and part notes to the kit. A section draws a
collapsible header with its name and a dim count, its header folds it, and every part starts at the
label's edge. A status row draws a mark in a fixed slot, the name, a detail line under it at the
same indent, and a state word; a row of a part that failed to refresh keeps its mark still. A part
note names the part, its age, and Retry, which reads the part again. The kit gallery's Content
section shows a pull request's checks, threads, and slices, live and stale, and folds each section.
